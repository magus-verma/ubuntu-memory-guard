# Code of conduct

Be respectful, specific, and constructive. Discuss ideas and technical evidence;
do not attack people. Harassment, threats, discrimination, and posting someone
else's private information are unacceptable.

Help newcomers understand requirements and validation limits. Maintainers may
edit or remove abusive content and restrict participation when necessary. Report
conduct concerns to the maintainer through an appropriate GitHub contact channel;
do not publish private evidence in an issue.
